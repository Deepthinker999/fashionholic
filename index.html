About

How this store works

Fashionholic is an editorial affiliate storefront, not a warehouse. We browse men's printed shirts, t-shirts, and casual wear, shortlist pieces we would actually wear, and publish them here with the current price we saw at the retailer. When you click through and buy, the retailer fulfils the order. We never handle your payment, size, or delivery.

We use affiliate links. If you purchase through a link we share, we may earn a commission from the retailer at no extra cost to you. That commission is how the site stays online. It does not change the price you pay, and it does not let us invent stock we do not have. If a link is an affiliate link, treat it as an advertisement for that product — still independently chosen, still honestly labelled.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Fashionholic is an independent editorial storefront that recommends men's clothing. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you visit this website. It is written to stand on its own: you should be able to read this section and know what we collect, what we do not, and how affiliate links work.

Affiliate links

Many of the product links on Fashionholic are affiliate links. If you click a link and then buy something from a retailer (for example Amazon or Flipkart), we may receive a commission at no extra cost to you. Affiliate partners may set their own cookies and collect data under their own privacy policies. We do not control those third-party sites, their checkout, or their advertising networks. Following an affiliate link is a visit to that retailer, not a purchase from Fashionholic.

What we do not collect

We do not operate a shopping cart, process payments, or collect credit-card, bank, UPI, or wallet details. Purchases are completed on the retailer's website. We do not create customer accounts on this site and we do not store shipping addresses, sizes, or order histories. If a retailer asks you to log in, that relationship is between you and them.

Cookies and analytics

This site may use basic cookies or similar technologies to remember simple preferences and to understand which pages are visited. If we use an analytics service, it typically records anonymous or aggregated information such as pages viewed, approximate region, device type, and referral source — not your name or payment details. You can block or delete cookies in your browser. The site remains readable without them. Third-party retailers you visit after clicking a link may set additional cookies; those are governed by the retailer, not by us.

Email you send us

If you write to contact@fashionholic.example, we use that correspondence only to reply. We do not sell, rent, or trade your contact details. We keep emails as long as needed to resolve your request, then delete or archive them in the ordinary course of running a small editorial site.

Changes and questions

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always published on this page with the date above. Questions about privacy, cookies, or affiliate disclosure can be sent to contact@fashionholic.example. We will answer as promptly as a small team can.
